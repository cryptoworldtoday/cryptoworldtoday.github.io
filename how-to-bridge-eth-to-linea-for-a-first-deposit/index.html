How to Bridge ETH to Linea for a First Deposit

For your first bridge, add Linea to MetaMask, use the official bridge to deposit ETH from Ethereum, and wait for ETH to appear at the same address.

By The Coin Wire EditorialNov 27, 2026 · 04:41 UTC4 min read

After a first transfer, ETH is usable on Linea when you add Linea to MetaMask and deposit ETH from Ethereum through the official Linea bridge. Linea is a separate network, so ETH on Ethereum must be moved there before it can pay Linea gas or be used in a swap.

A bridge locks ETH on Ethereum and credits ETH on Linea. Before the deposit, your ETH is on Ethereum; afterward, the credited ETH appears on Linea at the same wallet address. Your MetaMask address stays the same, but the network showing its balance changes.

Bridge ETH before any other token: gas on Linea is paid in ETH, so tokens that arrive without ETH can be stuck until you fund the wallet with ETH. Once the ETH arrives, it can cover gas for a first swap on SyncSwap, a decentralized exchange protocol for trading tokens and accessing liquidity pools on supported networks. Treat that as a later use of the ETH; the first task is making sure some remains available for gas.

What to have ready

For a first Linea deposit, have MetaMask, ETH on Ethereum mainnet, and enough ETH to cover both the amount you want to bridge and Ethereum transaction gas. The deposit is an Ethereum transaction, so the wallet needs ETH on Ethereum to submit it. The ETH you bridge becomes available on Linea after the bridge completes; it cannot also pay the Ethereum transaction fee.

Decide on a small test deposit before moving the rest. The test lets you confirm that you selected the right destination network and wallet address, and that ETH appears where expected. The test itself still requires Ethereum gas, so weigh the reassurance against paying for an additional transaction.

How to deposit ETH from Ethereum to Linea

MetaMask and the official Linea bridge move ETH from Ethereum to Linea in a single deposit flow. Follow the wallet prompts carefully: the wallet signs the Ethereum transaction, while the bridge handles crediting ETH on Linea.

  1. Add Linea to MetaMask. In MetaMask, add or select the Linea network. Its mainnet chain ID is 59144. The visible result is Linea available as a network in the wallet; adding it does not move ETH or change the balance on Ethereum.
  2. Open the official Linea bridge. Navigate to the bridge from Linea’s official site, then connect MetaMask. The bridge should show your connected wallet and let you choose Ethereum as the source and Linea as the destination. Connecting a wallet does not itself transfer funds.
  3. Choose ETH and enter a small test amount. Set the asset to ETH, confirm the direction is Ethereum to Linea, and enter the amount. Review the amount, destination address, and any fee or transaction details shown before continuing. The preview is your chance to catch a wrong network or amount before a transaction is submitted.
  4. Confirm the deposit in MetaMask. MetaMask will show an Ethereum transaction for your approval, including its gas cost. Confirm only after reviewing the details. The visible result is a submitted transaction; the ETH may not appear on Linea immediately while the transaction and bridge processing complete.
  5. Switch MetaMask to Linea and check the balance. After the deposit completes, select Linea in MetaMask and look for the credited ETH at the same address. The task is done when the ETH balance appears on Linea. If you used a test amount, you can repeat the bridge steps for the rest, leaving enough ETH on Linea for gas.

The important before-and-after check is the network name and balance, not a new wallet address. Before, MetaMask showed ETH on Ethereum; after, it should show ETH on Linea. The original Ethereum balance will reflect the deposit and its transaction cost, while the Linea balance provides funds for Linea transactions.

Related

How long does bridging to Linea take?

Bridging to Linea takes the time needed for the Ethereum transaction to confirm and for the bridge to complete its processing. The exact wait can vary, so use the transaction status in the bridge or wallet rather than assuming that a submitted transaction means the Linea balance is ready. Check the Linea balance before trying to trade or send the ETH.

What does it cost to bridge to Linea?

The cost includes Ethereum gas for the deposit transaction, and the bridge may show other transaction details before you confirm. Ethereum gas varies with network conditions, so read the current MetaMask quote before approving. After the ETH arrives, keep some on Linea for gas: Linea transactions, including swaps, also use ETH to pay fees.

Once the test deposit appears on Linea, decide whether to bridge the remaining ETH, keeping a portion available for gas before making your first swap.
