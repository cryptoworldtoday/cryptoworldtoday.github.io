Editorial policy

Scope

The Coin Wire reports on Crypto: markets, exchanges, protocols, regulation and security. We explain what happened and why it matters. We do not publish trading signals, price targets or personal investment recommendations.

Sources

We work from primary material wherever it exists — company and project statements, regulatory documents, on-chain records — and from reputable reporting. Sources are linked in the text so readers can check them.

No endorsements

Our stories are written for readers, not for the projects they describe. A reference to any token, platform or service is information, not a recommendation.

Fixing mistakes

Accuracy matters more than speed. When an error of fact comes to light, the article is corrected.

Not financial advice

Our content is informational. It does not take anyone's personal circumstances into account and is not investment advice. Digital assets carry significant risk.
