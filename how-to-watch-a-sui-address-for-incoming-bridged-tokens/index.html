How to Watch a Sui Address for Incoming Bridged Tokens

By The Coin Wire EditorialNov 28, 2026 · 07:52 UTC2 min read

After a bridge transfer, watch the recipient’s Sui address on Suiscan or SuiVision to see when the bridged coin and amount appear. Bookmark the address page once, then reopen it after each transfer instead of repeatedly checking your wallet.

Have ready: the recipient Sui address and the bridged coin type—the identifier for the specific kind of coin arriving. Suibridge transfers supported assets from Ethereum to Sui through a lock-and-mint process, so the arrival appears as a coin of a particular type on the Sui address page. For a Suibridge transfer, keep the recipient address handy while you follow the steps below; the Suibridge link is useful when checking which bridge transfer you are tracking.

Watch the Sui address for a balance change

  1. Open Suiscan. Go to the Suiscan explorer, where Sui addresses and their activity are publicly viewable.
  2. Search the recipient address. Paste the full Sui address into the search field and open the matching address page. Confirm the page shows the recipient address you expect.
  3. Bookmark the address page. Save the page in your browser. It is public and read-only, so you can reopen it from any device without logging in, connecting a Sui wallet or signing a transaction.
  4. Refresh after Ethereum finality. Once the Ethereum side of the bridge transfer has reached finality, open the bookmark and refresh the address page. This gives the bridge time to complete the transfer to Sui.
  5. Open the newest balance change. Look in the address’s balance changes for the bridged coin type. Open the newest relevant entry and read the listed amount and transaction details. The coin and amount appearing in the address’s balance changes confirms the bridged arrival.

SuiVision offers another explorer where you can view address balance changes. The same routine applies: search the recipient address, bookmark its page, then return after Ethereum finality and look for the new balance entry for the bridged coin type. You can check the page from a phone or computer, and anyone with the public address can view it.

For a Suibridge transfer, use the recipient address page to confirm the Sui-side result: the bridged coin type, its amount and the transaction that added it.
