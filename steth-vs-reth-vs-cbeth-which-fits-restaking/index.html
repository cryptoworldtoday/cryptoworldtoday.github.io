stETH vs rETH vs cbETH: which fits restaking?

stETH vs rETH vs cbETH for restaking comes down to AVS acceptance, issuer trust and exits: stETH is the default, rETH favors permissionless operators, cbETH suits Coinbase exits.

By The Coin Wire EditorialNov 24, 2026 · 23:41 UTC5 min read

For most restakers, stETH—usually deposited as wstETH—is the default for its broad acceptance and exit liquidity; choose rETH for permissionless operators or cbETH if you prefer to exit through Coinbase.

Restaking adds another use for staked ETH: EigenLayer lets stakers help secure additional services, with potential rewards and slashing risk. The LST you deposit still brings its own issuer, liquidity and token mechanics into the position.

Before signing any deposit, make sure the wallet connection is genuine and the transaction matches your intent. Our explanation of how to connect a wallet safely before eigen layer signing adds useful context on checking that step.

Which differences should decide your restaking asset?

How do token mechanics affect the position?

stETH changes your displayed token balance as staking rewards accrue, while rETH and cbETH represent value through a rising exchange rate. Lido’s stETH rebases daily; wstETH wraps it into a token whose balance stays fixed as its claim on stETH grows. Rocket Pool’s rETH and Coinbase’s cbETH also accumulate staking value through their exchange rates.

That difference affects how a balance appears and how a contract handles it, but it does not by itself determine restaking rewards. Compare the amount of underlying ETH represented, rather than comparing token counts alone.

How do issuer structure and fees change the risk?

Lido uses a curated set of node operators, Rocket Pool uses permissionless operators who bond their own ETH alongside pooled deposits, and Coinbase is a single custodian for the ETH backing cbETH. These structures put different weight on operator selection, bonded capital and reliance on one company.

Lido takes 10% of staking rewards, split between node operators and the DAO treasury. That fee reduces the staking rewards accruing to stETH holders; it is separate from any restaking rewards. For rETH, consider Rocket Pool’s operator and protocol economics alongside the permissionless model. For cbETH, weigh Coinbase’s custody role against your preference for an exchange-issued asset.

Which asset has the exit route you need?

stETH and wstETH can be redeemed for ETH through Lido’s withdrawal queue, subject to the queue and Ethereum’s withdrawal process. rETH holders can use Rocket Pool’s burn route or sell through a decentralized exchange. cbETH holders can redeem through Coinbase or sell on a decentralized exchange.

Those routes differ in how directly they depend on the issuer and on market liquidity. A DEX sale offers a market exit, but the price can differ from the token’s underlying ETH value. If you may need to leave restaking quickly, consider the route you would actually use and its current liquidity.

Will the AVSs pay rewards on your asset?

AVS rewards are set per strategy, and each AVS sets the multiplier for that strategy. That means equal ETH value in rETH and stETH can earn different AVS rewards if the operator’s AVSs accept different strategies or apply different multipliers.

Say you deposit rETH, but an AVS your operator secures accepts only a stETH strategy: that AVS pays no rewards on your rETH. An accepted strategy with a lower multiplier can also earn less than another accepted strategy with a higher one. Before choosing by headline reward rates, look up which strategies the operator’s AVSs accept and the multiplier assigned to each. Acceptance and multipliers can differ across AVSs, so they are practical signals to recheck.

What does changing LST later involve?

Switching from one restaked LST to another means completing an EigenLayer withdrawal, swapping the asset, then depositing and delegating again. That adds time and transactions, so make the choice with the likely exit route and AVS strategy acceptance in mind before depositing.

Who is each asset best suited to?

Choose stETH or wstETH if you want the broadest acceptance and deepest exit liquidity. Choose rETH if permissionless operators and their bonded ETH matter most to you. Choose cbETH if Coinbase is your preferred route back to ETH or fiat. The key check before depositing is whether your operator’s AVSs accept the asset’s strategy and what multiplier they assign.

Related questions

Is wstETH treated differently from stETH when restaking?

wstETH is a wrapped form of stETH, so its balance stays constant while its value in stETH grows. Whether an AVS strategy accepts it is a separate matter: verify the exact strategy and token accepted before depositing.

Can I switch restaked rETH to stETH without withdrawing first?

No; switching requires an EigenLayer withdrawal, a swap from rETH to stETH, and a new deposit and delegation. Plan for the withdrawal process before choosing rETH if you expect to need another asset.

What to check next

How long does the EigenLayer withdrawal delay last?

The delay depends on the withdrawal path and current protocol parameters, so check the current EigenLayer withdrawal interface and documentation when you initiate a withdrawal. Treat the withdrawal as a period when the position is unavailable, rather than assuming a fixed instant exit.

How do I convert stETH to wstETH before depositing?

Use Lido’s wrapping route to exchange stETH for wstETH, then confirm that the strategy you plan to use accepts wstETH. Wrapping changes the token representation; it does not remove the need to check strategy eligibility.

Which AVSs currently accept rETH as a strategy?

Acceptance varies by AVS and strategy, so check the operator’s current AVS strategy list before depositing. Confirm both that rETH is accepted and the multiplier the AVS assigns; if either is unsuitable, compare an accepted strategy before committing.

How does Rocket Pool’s commission affect rETH yield?

Rocket Pool node operators charge commission on staking rewards, which affects the net rewards accruing to rETH. Consider that staking yield alongside any AVS rewards and multiplier; the latter depends on the AVS strategy, not just the LST.
