About The Coin Wire

The Coin Wire reports on the crypto market as a whole: Bitcoin and Ethereum, layer-2 networks, DeFi protocols, stablecoins, exchanges and the regulation that shapes them. Each story sets a development against what came before and against the alternatives, then names what to watch next.

We are independent and not affiliated with any project, token or company we cover. We work from primary sources such as filings, governance proposals, code repositories and official statements, and we correct errors openly in the article where they occurred. Nothing published here is financial advice.
